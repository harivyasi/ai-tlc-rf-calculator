Instructions
How to analyse your plate
  1. Load a plate image from the ELN or by uploading a local file.
  2. Set the Min Line (baseline / origin) and the Max Line (solvent front).
  3. Click Add Spots and mark each spot, or use Auto-detect to find candidates automatically, then adjust.
  4. Optionally add V-Line / H-Line labels to name lanes and compounds.
  5. Click Calculate RF. Results appear in the table below; export as CSV or send back to the ELN.

Tip: drag spots or lines to reposition, and double-click a spot to delete it. RF values should fall between 0 and 1.
